PRIVACY

Privacy information

Owner review required before launch. This is a starter disclosure, not a finished legal policy. Update it to reflect the practitioner’s real booking provider, payment provider, communications, data retention, and Indian legal obligations. Get professional advice when appropriate.

We respect the privacy of people making enquiries and appointments. This website itself is a static information site. It does not include a clinical intake form, patient portal, advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Information collected by external services

When you use the booking tool, the scheduling provider may collect your name, contact details, appointment selection and any information you choose to enter on its page. If you pay online, the payment service processes the payment. The practice should state the providers it uses and link their privacy notices here: [ADD BOOKING PROVIDER] and [ADD PAYMENT PROVIDER].

Why information is used

Appointment information may be used to confirm, manage, reschedule and communicate about appointments, and to handle payments if applicable. Any additional purposes should be described here before going live.

Access, retention and requests

The practice owner should explain who can access appointment records, how long data is retained, and how someone can request access or correction. Do not publish patient files or clinical notes on GitHub Pages. [ADD ACTUAL RETENTION AND REQUEST PROCESS].

Third-party embeds and cookies

Embedded booking or payment tools may use cookies or similar technology subject to their own policies. Their actual behaviour depends on the services the practice connects. [ADD ACTUAL PROVIDER DETAILS].
