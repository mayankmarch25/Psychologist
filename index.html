GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions.

Not sure where to start? These answers may help you decide what your next step looks like.

What happens in a first session? +

The first session is generally an opportunity to discuss what has brought you here, ask questions and understand whether the approach feels right for you. Exact arrangements will be explained by the practitioner.

Can I book an online appointment? +

Appointment formats are listed in the booking information. Please confirm availability before booking.

How are fees and payments handled? +

Fees will be shown or confirmed before an appointment is finalised. If payments are enabled in the booking tool, the checkout is handled by that provider.

What if I need to reschedule? +

Cancellation and rescheduling terms will be shared before your appointment is confirmed.

Is this an emergency service? +

No. This website and its booking tools are not monitored for emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger in India, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.
